OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

The Story Behind Every Cup & Plate

L'Aurora was born from a singular passion: elevating daily cafe rituals into sensory art. Founded in Tribeca in 2018, we bridge micro-lot roasting with chef-driven farm-to-table cuisine.

♧   Ethically Sourced Coffee Beans

We partner directly with smallholder farmers in Colombia and Ethiopia, paying 2x fair trade minimums.

♨   Daily 5:00 AM Sourdough & Pastry Bake

Wild fermented sourdough loaves and laminated AOP French butter pastries rolled fresh every morning.

✧   0% Artificial Additives or Preservatives

Clean whole ingredients, natural honeys, unrefined sugars, and organic dairy from local regenerative farms.
